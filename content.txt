## 1. Project Lore

The signal came through at 0347 Zulu, bounced off a decommissioned Soviet satellite and into a receiver buried beneath the Swiss Alps. By 0400, seventeen world governments had been notified. By 0430, all seventeen had issued denial orders. The footage—grainy thermal imaging of craft moving at impossible angles over the Atacama—was scrubbed, reclassified, buried. But someone saved a copy. Someone always saves a copy. That someone became Commander Zero, and XCOM was activated from dormancy protocols written during the Cold War, funded by dead men's money, and staffed by ghosts.

We operate from black-sites in places that don't exist on civilian maps. Abandoned mining facilities in Nevada. Derelict oil platforms in the North Sea. Former Soviet bunkers reclaimed from radioactive exclusion zones. Each token holder is assigned a designation, a clearance level, and a purpose. This isn't a community—it's a unit. There are no influencers here. No hype men. Only operators, analysts, engineers, and the cold mathematics of species survival. The aliens have been conducting reconnaissance for eighteen months. Abductions are up 340%. Governments remain paralyzed. XCOM does not.

Every buy is a requisition order. Every holder is enlisted personnel defending the Blue Marble against threats that official channels refuse to acknowledge. The war room monitors are always lit. The interceptors are always fueled. And when contact is confirmed—when the skies go wrong and radar stations start screaming—we are the ones who answer. Not because we chose this. Because no one else will. Vigilo Confido. We watch. We trust. We are humanity's last line.
